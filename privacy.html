Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Content Studio is a private application used only by its owner, Adele Cooper, to manage her own social media accounts (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube). It has no other users and is not available to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Content Studio reads data from the owner's own accounts: post and video statistics, YouTube Analytics reports, and comments and messages on her own posts. It does not access anyone else's account.

How data is stored

Data is stored only on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google user data

Content Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used only to show the owner her own channel's statistics. The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
